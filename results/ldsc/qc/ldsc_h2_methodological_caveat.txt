Univariate LDSC h2 methodological caveat
=======================================

These analyses are an observed-scale diagnostic/QC stage. Binary traits are not transformed to the liability scale, and no sample or population prevalence is supplied.

FinnGen summary statistics were produced using REGENIE. The FinnGen R12 quantitative laboratory documentation specifically describes a linear mixed-model framework. LDSC documentation cautions that mixed-model summary statistics may have effective sample sizes that differ from nominal sample sizes, which can affect absolute SNP-heritability estimates.

Accordingly:
- The requested univariate LDSC outputs are preserved and used primarily as diagnostics/readiness checks.
- R12 laboratory h2 estimates must not be described as definitive biological SNP heritability estimates.
- Absolute h2 values should not be compared naively across traits.
- No alternative effective sample size is estimated or substituted.
- This caveat does not automatically invalidate later genetic-correlation analysis, because multiplicative sample-size scaling can cancel in rg, but future rg still requires appropriate h2/readiness review.

The LDSC intercept is estimated freely. Neither --no-intercept nor --intercept-h2 is used.
